<section id="multimodal-deep-learning-research" class="project">
## Multimodal Deep Learning Research
<p class="meta">PhD Research and Publications</p>
<div class="tags"><span class="tag">Graph Neural Networks</span><span class="tag">Multimodal AI</span><span class="tag">Real-time Prediction</span></div>

- Built MSGNN, a multi-structured graph neural network for predicting traffic incidents across large road networks.
- Developed M2NN, a multi-view, multi-task model combining sensor data and congestion heatmap imagery.
- Evaluated both approaches across Brisbane and Gold Coast networks at 15-minute and 60-minute prediction horizons.
- Published the work open access in Transportation Research Part C.
